SECURE DATA ARCHITECTURE

Privacy Policy & Data Sovereignty

Last Updated: October 8, 2026 • Autonomous Self-Discipline Protocol

1. Autonomous On-Device Parsing (UPI & Bank SMS)

Sanchay utilizes an on-device Android NotificationListenerService and local regex state machine to detect financial debits and credits from payment applications (including Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM) and bank transactional SMS messages.

Crucial Guarantee: All SMS and notification text parsing occurs strictly locally on your mobile device. Raw SMS contents, bank OTPs, personal messages, and personal identity credentials never leave your local hardware environment.

2. Zero Personal Credential Transmission

Sanchay never asks for, captures, logs, or transmits:

  • Your Bank Account Logins, Passwords, or NetBanking Credentials.
  • Your UPI PIN, ATM PIN, or Debit/Credit Card CVV numbers.
  • One-Time Passwords (OTPs) or Two-Factor Authentication codes.

Sanchay is an informational expense ledger and behavioral pacing tool. It is not connected via account aggregator banking APIs and does not hold custody of your funds.

3. AES-256 On-Device Storage & Local Data Sovereignty

All transacted amounts, merchant metadata, and category tags are saved into your private local SQLite database on your device and synchronized strictly with your authenticated Google Firebase account via TLS 1.3 encryption.

You maintain 100% data sovereignty. At any point, you can:

  • Export your entire transaction ledger into standard RFC 4180 Excel CSV format.
  • Generate an AES-256 encrypted JSON backup file for cold offline storage.
  • Execute a single-tap Purge All Data command which permanently deletes all records from local storage and cloud database.

4. Zero Third-Party Advertising & Zero Data Selling

We believe financial intelligence must remain completely private. Sanchay will never sell, rent, monetize, or broker your financial transactions or shopping habits to ad networks, insurance providers, credit score companies, or data brokers.

5. Sanchay Connect & Friend Network Privacy

When using Sanchay Connect to chat with peers or coordinate shared goal pacing:

  • You only connect with users whom you explicitly accept via bidirectional friend requests.
  • You may block any user at any time, instantly preventing discovery and messaging.
  • The Delete for Everyone feature physically erases the message document from the secure cloud thread.
