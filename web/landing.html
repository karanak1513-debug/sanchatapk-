12 — Clarity

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Sanchat's security, tracking, and Google Play compliance.

How does UPI tracking work without bank logins? +
Sanchat parses standard transaction alerts received from UPI apps (PhonePe, Google Pay, Paytm) strictly on your device using pattern matching. It does not require your bank password, debit card, or MPIN.
Are my personal messages or OTPs ever read? +
No. Sanchat filters for specific financial debit/credit triggers (e.g. "credited with INR", "debited by ₹"). OTP messages and personal chat threads are immediately ignored and never transmitted.
Can I permanently delete my account and data? +
Yes. Directly in Settings > Danger Zone, tap "Delete Account Permanently". This permanently wipes your Firebase Auth account, cloud vaults, and transaction records from the database in compliance with Google Play mandates.
Why is the APK targeted at Android 16 (API 36)? +
Sanchat is engineered to meet Google Play's latest SDK requirements (`targetSdk = 36`), guaranteeing support for the latest Android platform security, permissions architecture, and background service policies.
